About

I started in tech in 2006 as a support intern and worked through every layer of software – testing, coding, leading projects, managing teams. In 2013 I founded Berni Software, and since 2019 I have been CTO of Boxifarma, responsible for technology strategy, architecture and engineering. Having done the work myself, I lead from experience.

In aviation, I have spent more than a decade in the profession – first as a flight instructor, and since 2018 as an airline pilot at LATAM Airlines, flying the Airbus A320 family. My first period there was interrupted by the pandemic in 2020; in 2022 I proudly returned and resumed the journey.

Each career makes me better at the other. Aviation gave my technology leadership discipline, procedure and calm under pressure. Technology gave my flying an analytical, systems-oriented mindset.

What the cockpit taught my engineering

  1. Standard procedures

    Checklists and SOPs become runbooks, reviews and automated pipelines: delivery that is repeatable, not heroic.

  2. Crew resource management

    Teams where anyone can speak up, roles are clear and the best information wins, not the highest rank.

  3. Decisions under pressure

    Calm, structured incident response: assess, decide, communicate, then review what happened.

  4. Safety as a non-negotiable

    Reliability and quality designed in from the start, because in both worlds failure is expensive.
